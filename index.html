A simplified 5-rule cheat sheet that captures ~90% of full basic strategy's value with far less to memorize. Source: Kevin Stone. Rule 5 uses surrender, which isn't assumed elsewhere on this site — treat these 5 rules as a standalone quick-start, not a supplement to the full chart.

  1. Stand Hard 12–16 vs dealer < 7
  2. Double Hard 9–11 vs dealer < 9
  3. Split 2,2 · 3,3 · 6,6 · 7,7 · 8,8 · A,A vs any dealer card
  4. Double Soft 13–18 vs dealer 4–6
  5. Surrender Hard 15–16 vs dealer 10 or A

Everything else: hit.
