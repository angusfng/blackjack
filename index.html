A simplified 5-rule cheat sheet that captures ~90% of full basic strategy's value with far less to memorize. Source: Kevin Stone. These 5 rules are a standalone quick-start, not a supplement to the full chart (Rule 5 uses surrender, which the rest of this site doesn't assume).

Quick glossary: "hard" total = no ace, or an ace counting as 1 (e.g. 10+6 = hard 16). "soft" total = a hand with an ace counting as 11 (e.g. A+7 = soft 18). Dealer card = the one face-up card you can see.

  1. Stand
    Your hand totals 12–16 (hard), and dealer shows 2–6 e.g. you have 10+5 = 16, dealer shows a 4 → stay put, the dealer is likely to bust.
  2. Double
    Your hand totals 9, 10, or 11 (hard), and dealer shows 2–8 e.g. you have 6+4 = 10, dealer shows a 7 → double your bet, take exactly one more card.
  3. Split
    You're dealt a pair of 2s, 3s, 6s, 7s, 8s, or Aces — any dealer card e.g. you have 8+8, no matter what the dealer shows → split into two separate hands.
  4. Double
    Your hand is soft 13–18 (an ace + a 2–7), and dealer shows 4, 5, or 6 e.g. you have A+6 = soft 17, dealer shows a 5 → double your bet, take one more card.
  5. Surrender
    Your hand totals 15 or 16 (hard), and dealer shows a 10-value card or Ace e.g. you have 10+6 = 16, dealer shows a King → give up half your bet and end the hand, if your casino allows it.

Anything not covered above: hit.
